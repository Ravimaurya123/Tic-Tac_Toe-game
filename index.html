How to Play

  • The game is played on a 3x3 grid.
  • Choose between 2 Players or vs Computer AI mode.
  • Player 1 uses X (Neon Red) and plays first.
  • Player 2 / Computer uses O (Electric Cyan).
  • Players take turns placing their symbols on empty cells.
  • Align three symbols in a row, column, or diagonal to win!
